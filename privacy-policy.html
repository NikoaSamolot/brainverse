PRIVACY POLICY – BRAINVERSE

Last updated: 7 September 2026

1. GENERAL INFORMATION

Brainverse is a web-based platform and may also be accessed through applications using WebView.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when using the Brainverse website and its games.

Brainverse is designed to process only information that is necessary for the operation, security, and functionality of its services.

Brainverse does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal data to companies, advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Brainverse does not use user data for advertising, targeted advertising, user profiling, or data brokerage.

2. TECHNICAL INFORMATION

When using Brainverse, certain basic technical information may be automatically processed as part of normal Internet communication and the operation and security of the website.

Depending on the connection and service being used, this may include:

  • IP address;
  • browser or device information;
  • connection information;
  • session information; and
  • technical server logs.

Such information may be necessary to:

  • deliver and operate the website;
  • establish and maintain network connections;
  • maintain service security;
  • prevent abuse or disruption;
  • diagnose technical problems; and
  • keep the service functioning properly.

Brainverse does not intentionally collect or use technical information for advertising, targeted advertising, profiling, data brokerage, selling user data, or other purposes unrelated to operating and securing its services.

Technical information may also be automatically handled by infrastructure providers as part of normal Internet communication and hosting. Such technical processing is separate from Brainverse intentionally collecting information for its own unrelated purposes.

3. ONLINE GAMES AND RENDER

Some Brainverse games use Render as their hosting and infrastructure provider for online multiplayer functionality.

For these games, the game application running on Render stores only information required by the game to provide its multiplayer functionality.

This may include:

  • the nickname selected by the player;
  • the lobby in which the player is currently participating; and
  • current gameplay information, such as the player's score or other statistics during a round.

The nickname is used as an in-game identifier. It does not constitute a Brainverse account and does not create a personal profile.

Brainverse does not require users to create a Render account to participate in these games.

The game application does not use the information described above for advertising, targeted advertising, marketing, profiling, data brokerage, or the sale of user information.

The information stored by the game on Render is used solely to operate the relevant game and provide its multiplayer functionality.

Render or other infrastructure used to provide the service may also technically receive information required for normal network communication, such as connection-related information. Such information is not part of the game's stored player data described above and is not intentionally used by Brainverse for unrelated purposes.

Games that do not require online functionality do not need to store multiplayer game data on Render.

4. NICKNAMES

Players may be asked to choose a nickname when participating in an online game.

The nickname is used to identify and display the player within the game.

Because a nickname may sometimes contain information that can identify a person, users should avoid including their real name, contact information, or other sensitive personal information in a nickname.

A nickname does not create a Brainverse account and does not by itself create a personal user profile.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Brainverse may use third-party infrastructure or technical services when they are necessary for the operation of the website or a particular game.

These services may technically process information required to provide the functionality for which they are used.

For example, Render may provide the hosting and infrastructure required for online multiplayer games.

Third-party processing may include information required for network communication, hosting, game sessions, or other technical functions.

Brainverse does not intentionally provide user personal data to third parties for their own advertising, marketing, profiling, data brokerage, or other unrelated commercial purposes.

The use of a third-party infrastructure provider for technical purposes does not mean that Brainverse sells or commercially shares user data.

6. DATA SHARING AND SALE

Brainverse does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal data.

Brainverse does not intentionally provide personal data to companies, advertisers, or other organizations for the purpose of:

  • advertising;
  • targeted advertising;
  • marketing;
  • user profiling;
  • data brokerage; or
  • using the data for their own unrelated commercial purposes.

However, information may necessarily be transmitted to or processed by technical service providers when this is required for the website or a particular game to function.

For example, a multiplayer game hosted on Render requires the game application and the hosting infrastructure to process the information necessary to maintain the game and its connections.

Such technical processing is performed to provide the Brainverse service and is not a sale or commercial transfer of user data.

7. PURPOSE OF DATA PROCESSING

Information processed in connection with Brainverse is used for purposes necessary to provide, operate, secure, and maintain the website and its games.

These purposes include:

  • operating the Brainverse website;
  • operating games;
  • providing online multiplayer functionality;
  • maintaining network connections and game sessions;
  • maintaining service security;
  • preventing abuse or disruption;
  • diagnosing technical problems; and
  • maintaining the reliability of the service.

Brainverse does not intentionally use information collected or processed through its services for purposes unrelated to the operation, security, or functionality of those services.

8. SECURITY

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed in connection with Brainverse.

However, no website, online service, or method of transmitting information over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should therefore avoid voluntarily providing sensitive or confidential personal information through Brainverse unless a particular feature requires it.

9. USER RIGHTS

Depending on the information concerned and applicable law, users may have rights regarding their personal data, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of applicable personal information.

Because Brainverse does not generally use user accounts or maintain personal profiles, the amount of personal information associated with an individual user may be limited.

If you have a question or request concerning information processed by Brainverse, you can contact the Brainverse administrator using the contact information below.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future to reflect changes to Brainverse, its games, or the services used to operate them.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will apply after it is published on the Brainverse website.

11. CONTACT

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about the handling of information by a particular Brainverse game, contact the Brainverse administrator at:

nikodem@brainverse.pl

12. SUMMARY

In simple terms:

  • Brainverse may process basic technical information necessary for the website and its games to function.
  • This may include information such as IP addresses, connection information, session information, or technical server logs.
  • Such technical information is used for normal operation, security, maintenance, and troubleshooting.
  • Some online games use Render as their hosting and infrastructure provider.
  • The game application running on Render stores only game information necessary for multiplayer functionality, such as a player's chosen nickname, current lobby, score, and other current round statistics.
  • A player's nickname is an in-game identifier and does not create a Brainverse or Render account.
  • Network infrastructure may technically process additional connection-related information as part of normal Internet communication and hosting.
  • Brainverse does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal data.
  • Brainverse does not intentionally provide user personal data to companies for advertising, targeted advertising, marketing, profiling, data brokerage, or their own unrelated commercial purposes.
  • Information may be processed by technical service providers when this is necessary to operate the website or a particular game.
  • Information stored by the game on Render is used solely to provide the relevant game's functionality.

END OF POLICY
