Privacy Policy – BrainVerse OFFLINE Games

BrainVerse OFFLINE Games respects your privacy.

This application does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

The application does not use user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, cookies, or external data collection services.

All games and core application features operate locally on your device. An internet connection is not required to play the games or access their core functionality.

The application may connect to Google Play services to check for available application updates. This functionality may process device metadata, application version information, and installed application modules to determine whether an update is available. This information is handled by Google Play for update functionality and is not used by BrainVerse for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Any game progress, preferences, or other locally stored information used by the application remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

We do not have access to information stored locally by the application.

BrainVerse does not sell or share users' personal information with third parties.
